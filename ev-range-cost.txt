Privacy Policy for EV Range Cost

Last updated: 13 October 2026

EV Range Cost ("the app") compares the running cost of petrol or diesel cars with electric cars.

Data we collect
===============
The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. The figures you enter (fuel prices, tank size, electricity rates, battery sizes, units and similar settings) are stored only on your own device, in the app's private storage. They are never sent to the developer or to any third party.

Accounts, ads and analytics
===========================
The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Permissions
===========
The app does not request any special permissions.

External links
==============
The Credits screen contains a link to a Buy Me a Coffee page. If you tap it, it opens in your web browser. That site is operated by a third party and has its own privacy policy. The app itself shares no information when you do this.

Children
========
The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data
==================
Your figures can be reset inside the app (menu → Reset to Defaults), and uninstalling the app removes all data it stored.

Changes
=======
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
